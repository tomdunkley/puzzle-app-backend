# dictionary.txt provenance

Source: ENABLE word list (Enhanced North American Benchmark LExicon), public domain.
Mirrored from https://norvig.com/ngrams/enable1.txt (172,819 words), filtered to
length >= 4 to match `MIN_WORD_LENGTH` in `app/services/boggle.py` (171,752 words).

Chosen over the previous dwyl/english-words list because it's specifically curated
for word games (Scrabble/Boggle clones), rather than a generic English dictionary
full of obscure technical/scientific terms.
